# Pilot usability study — not yet conducted

Recruit 5–10 consenting EV drivers. Do not collect identifiable billing or meter files for publication. The website analyses uploaded data locally. Use the synthetic CSV if the participant prefers.

Tasks: find the household bill calculator; calculate a charging plan; explain immediate/model/oracle differences; identify an infeasible energy target; inspect a forecast interval; load a CSV; enable the shared connection constraint; export and explain a schedule. Ask the participant which figure they would rely on and what they think the tool knows about tomorrow.

Record task completion, time, misinterpretations, failed imports and suggested label changes. Specifically test whether sampled-session savings are mistaken for annual savings, and whether forecast intervals are mistaken for guarantees. Test both German and English pages and phone layouts.

Pilot release gates: at least 80% complete the core planning task unaided; every participant can identify a target shortfall after explanation; no unresolved privacy or unit-confusion issue. These are proposed criteria, not achieved results. Publish only anonymised aggregates after consent. Household economics remain simulated until measured real-world outcomes exist.
